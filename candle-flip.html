How Candle Flip works

Every card carries a candlestick or chart pattern, and every pattern appears twice. Turn over two cards per move: a matching pair stays face up and names itself, anything else flips back. Clear the board to finish the run.

Practice: no clock, and the pattern name is printed on each card — the way to learn the shapes. Free, and not ranked.

Classic: the same board against the clock (Beginner 2:00, Intermediate 4:00, Advanced 6:00). Costs one life; ranked by score.

Blind: no names on the cards and mismatched pairs flip back faster, with no hints to lean on. Same clock as Classic. Costs one life; ranked by score.

Score: 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 base points by level, plus 50 for each pair, 20 for every card in your longest streak and 5 for each second left on the clock. Moves above par (2.5 per pair) cost 10 points each.

Streaks, coins and hints: three matches in a row make a streak. Clearing a ranked board earns 10 coins, plus 2 per streak and 5 if you never turned over a wrong pair. Spend 25 coins to peek at a card or add 30 seconds, 50 to reveal a whole pair.

Lives: you have up to 5. Classic and Blind cost one per run, and one comes back every 15 minutes.

Controls: click or tap the cards. P or Esc pauses, which also hides the board. M turns the sound on or off.

Pattern guide
