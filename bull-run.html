How Bull Run works

Each headline is a $1,000 virtual trade in the asset shown on the card. ▲ BUY profits if its price rose that day, ▼ SELL profits if it fell, by the real percentage move. Not sure? ● PASS skips the round: no gain, no loss, no points.

Run into the lane you pick before you reach the news wall. You start with $10,000 of virtual capital: a right call grows it by the real price move, a wrong call shrinks it. Your capital is your score on the leaderboard.

Practice: 8 headlines, and you see the correct answer after each one.

Survival: you have 3 lives and 2 passes, and you get less time with every headline. A wrong call costs a life. Staying in the PASS lane uses a pass; once your passes are used up, reaching the wall without picking BUY or SELL costs a life. Every 8 right calls in a row win a life back.

Controls: ← → or A / D to switch lanes, 1 2 3 to jump to a lane, P or Esc to pause. On touch screens, swipe or tap the lane buttons.
