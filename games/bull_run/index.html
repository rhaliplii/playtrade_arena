How the game works

Each headline is a $1,000 virtual trade in the asset shown on the card. BUY profits if its price rose that day, SELL profits if it fell, by the real percentage move. Traders call these going long and going short. Not sure? PASS to skip the round: no gain, no loss, no points. Right calls score 100 points, plus a bonus for deciding fast and for streaks.

Survival: a wrong call costs a life, and so does reaching the wall without picking a lane once your passes are used up, and you get less time with every headline. You have 2 passes: each PASS skips a round without losing a life, but resets your streak. With no passes left, PASS is locked. Every 8 right calls in a row win a life back. The explanations for your misses are waiting at the end.
