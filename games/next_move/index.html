How to play

Every round shows a chart with the next candles hidden. Read the setup and make one call.

↓ DownThe setup favours lower prices.
— No tradeNo clear edge. Passing is a real answer, and often the right one.
↑ UpThe setup favours higher prices.
  • Lives Survival gives you three. A wrong call costs one; at zero the run ends.
  • Timer Every chart has a clock, and harder charts give you less of it. Running out counts as a miss.
  • Streak Consecutive correct calls raise your multiplier, up to ×2.
  • Scoring Harder charts pay more, a quick answer adds a little, and a correct NO TRADE earns a discipline bonus.
  • Power-ups Earned as you play: an extra clue, more time, 50/50, a shield, a streak saver.
  • Practice No lives, a longer clock, three observations on every chart, and you can replay any of them.

The hidden candles reveal as soon as you choose, and the reason is explained straight away. Only the tools a lesson needs are drawn — nothing is on the chart for decoration. Markets are probabilistic: the goal is reading setups well, not being right every time.

Educational game using constructed, simulated scenarios. Charts carry real symbols so they read naturally, but the price action is invented to teach a concept — it is not historical or live market data, and nothing here is investment advice.
