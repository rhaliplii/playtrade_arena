How Next Move works

Each round shows a real chart with the next candles hidden. Read the chart and indicators, then make your call:

  • UP — Price will rise
  • DOWN — Price will fall
  • NO TRADE — No clear direction

The hidden candles then play out and you see what happened and why.

No Trade is a real answer. If there’s no clear setup, choosing No Trade scores like any other correct call.

Practice: No lives, more time, hints and power-ups. Free and unranked.

Survival: A wrong call costs 1 life; the run ends when all are gone.

Scoring: Correct calls earn points, harder charts score more, and streaks increase your multiplier. Finish a ranked run for +10 coins, +2 per streak and +5 for no wrong calls.

Lives: Hold up to 5 Arena lives. One refills every 15 min. Practice never costs a life.

Controls: ↑/W Up · ↓/S Down · Space No Trade · 1–5 Power-ups · P Pause
