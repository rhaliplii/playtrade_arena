How learning works

  1. 1

    Short lessons

    Rico explains an idea in a few cards, then you try it and answer 3 quick questions.

  2. 2

    Rico's Gym

    Questions you miss come back until you get them right. Get one right on 2 different days and it's mastered.

  3. 3

    Chapter Challenge

    A short quiz at the end of each chapter: one question per lesson, in new situations. Miss at most one to unlock the next chapter.

  4. 4

    Finish the path

    Pass every Challenge, get your personal trading plan and collect your certificate.

Common questions

Can I lose XP or my rank?

No. XP only goes up. Only the monthly Top Learners board resets, on the 1st.

Can I buy XP or a rank?

No. Rank looks are earned only. Coins buy game lives, tournament entries and accessories, never progress.

What if I fail a Chapter Challenge?

Nothing is lost. Take a quick refresher and retry with new questions, as often as you like.

How does my streak work?

Meet your daily goal (1, 2 or 4 lessons, a Gym session counts too) and your streak grows by a day. 7, 30, 100 and 365 days earn +100, +250, +500 and +1,000 XP.

What if I miss a day?

A streak freeze covers it. You earn one for every 7 days in a row and can hold 2. Weekend mode, in Settings, means Saturdays and Sundays never break your streak.

How does Top Learners work?

It ranks the XP everyone earned this calendar month and starts again on the 1st. You can hide yourself from it in Settings.
