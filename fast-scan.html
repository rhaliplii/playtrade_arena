How Fast Scan works

A grid of shuffled numbers appears. Tap them in ascending order, from the smallest to the largest, as fast as you can. A wrong tap is a mistake; the right one lights up and you move on.

Practice: numbers 1…N, no clock and no mistake limit. The next number is always shown. Free, and not ranked.

Classic: numbers 1…N against the clock, with a mistake limit that grows with the grid (3×3: 8s and 3 mistakes, up to 10×10: 120s and 7). Costs one life; ranked by time.

Hardcore: random numbers with gaps (for example 7, 19, 52, 88), no next-number hint and no hint buttons. Same clock and limits as Classic. Costs one life; ranked by time.

Streaks, coins and hints: right taps less than 2 seconds apart build a streak. Clearing a ranked board earns 10 coins, plus 2 for every streak of 3 or more and 5 for a perfect board. Spend 25 coins to reveal the next number or add 3 seconds.

Lives: you have up to 5. Classic and Hardcore cost one per run, and one comes back every 15 minutes.

Controls: click or tap the numbers. P or Esc pauses, which also hides the board. M turns the sound on or off.
